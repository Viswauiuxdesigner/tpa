About

Who we are, and why.

A professional association built by and for the photographers and videographers of Tirupur.

History

Our story.

Preserving the timeless craft of photography since 1998.

Established in 1998, the Tirupur Photography Association was formed by pioneer studio practitioners and darkroom printers to build unity, promote technical knowledge, and establish fair commercial standards across Tirupur's visual arts community.

Over the past three decades, the association has grown from a modest gathering of local portraitists into a premier guild representing more than 450 member studios, wedding cinematographers, wildlife observers, and freelance visual artists throughout the Kongu region.

Through rapid technological transitions — from medium-format film and chemical baths to full-frame mirrorless sensors and drone cinematography — TPA has consistently served as the guiding compass for Tirupur's image-makers.

Mission & vision

To empower, connect and elevate the photography and videography profession across Tirupur.

Our Mission

To unite all visual practitioners under one trusted guild; champion professional ethics; provide continuous technical education; and protect the economic welfare of every member studio and freelancer.

Our Vision

A vibrant, highly skilled, and technologically advanced photographic community in Tirupur recognized nationally for artistic integrity, commercial excellence, and fraternal support.
