Membership

Join the association.

Four membership categories. One registration form. Choose the one that fits how you work.

Fees are in Indian rupees. Annual plans are valid for one year from approval.

Life Membership

Renewal

Renewal & validity.

When does my membership need renewing?
Annual memberships are valid for 12 months from the approval date. Renewal notifications are sent via SMS and WhatsApp 30 days prior to expiration.
How do I renew?
Existing members can select the "Studio Renewal" plan on the registration page and input their existing member ID and payment reference.
Does Life Membership expire?
Life Membership requires a single one-time contribution and never expires, maintaining active directory listing and voting rights permanently.
